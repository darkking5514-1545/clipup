1Introduction

RH Studio PK (“we”, “us”, “our”) built ClipUp (package name com.rhstudio.clipup), an Android video editing app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app uses, how it is used and what choices you have. By using ClipUp you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

2Information We Collect / Access

We do not require you to create an account and we do not ask for your name, phone number or address. The app may use the following:

  • Your media files: videos, photos and audio that you choose to import so you can edit them. Editing happens on your device.
  • Device and usage information: such as device model, Android version, app version, language, crash logs and general interaction data, collected through Google/Firebase services to keep the app secure and stable.
  • Advertising identifiers: Google AdMob may use your Advertising ID and similar data to show ads.
  • AI feature inputs: if you use an AI feature, the text prompt or content you submit for that feature may be sent to Google’s AI services (Firebase AI) to produce a result.

3Permissions the App Requests

  • Photos, videos & audio (READ_MEDIA_*, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): to let you pick media to edit.
  • Storage (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, Android 9 and below): to save your exported videos.
  • Internet: to show ads, run AI features, security checks and app updates.

You can withdraw any permission at any time in your device settings; some features may then stop working.

4How We Use Information

  • To provide, run and improve video editing, export and AI features.
  • To show advertisements and measure their performance.
  • To protect the app against abuse, fraud and tampering (for example via Firebase App Check).
  • To fix bugs, analyse crashes and improve performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5On-Device Processing

Video editing, exporting and features such as background/person segmentation (Google ML Kit) run locally on your device. Your original and exported videos are stored on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

6Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & AdMob – policies.google.com/privacy
  • Firebase (AI, App Check, analytics/crash reporting) – firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google ML Kit – ML Kit data disclosure

7Advertising

We show ads through Google AdMob. Ads may be personalised based on your Advertising ID. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Google → Ads. Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), consent is requested before personalised ads are shown.

8Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers listed above, when required by law, or to protect our rights and users’ safety.

9Data Retention & Deletion

Your media stays on your device and you can delete it at any time. Data processed by Google/Firebase services is kept according to their retention policies. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored app data. To ask us to delete any data linked to you, email us at the address below.

10Data Security

We use reasonable technical measures, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections, to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11Children’s Privacy

ClipUp is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

12Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your data, to withdraw consent, and to object to personalised advertising. Contact us to exercise these rights.

13Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
